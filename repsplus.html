Reps+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Overview

Reps+ is a free iOS app for tracking workout progress and analyzing fitness activities. The app is designed for local workout tracking with different modi.

Data Collection

Reps+ collects no data at all.

  • No personal data is collected.
  • No usage or gameplay data is collected.
  • No analytics data is collected.
  • No device identifiers are collected.
  • No location data is collected.

Tracking and Advertising

Reps+ uses no tracking technologies, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics tools.

Accounts and Network Services

Reps+ does not require user accounts and does not send data to external servers.

Data Sharing

Since Reps+ collects no data, there is no data to sell, share, or disclose.

Policy Changes

If Reps+ privacy practices change in the future, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.
