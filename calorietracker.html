Calorie Tracker

An offline-first nutrition and training tracker for iOS and Android — calorie, weight and water logging, a recipe catalogue, workouts, and an optional accountability feature for sharing progress with a friend.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Calorie Tracker is offline-first: everything you log — meals, weight, water, workouts, recipes, streaks and achievements — is stored locally on your device.

  • We do not sell your personal data or show ads.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Searching for food by name or barcode sends that search term to Open Food Facts, an open, third-party product database, to look up nutrition information. Results are cached on your device so repeated searches keep working offline.

Buddy — optional accountability feature

Buddy lets you add a friend and compare progress. It is entirely opt-in — the app works fully without ever signing in.

  • Signing in only requires an email address, used solely to authenticate you.
  • Only aggregate, non-identifying stats are shared with a buddy who accepts your request — never your raw food diary or per-meal data. Your daily calories are never shown as an absolute number, and your macros are blended into a single figure.
  • If you allow notifications, a push token (Apple or Google) is stored so we can notify you about buddy requests, challenge activity and weekly checkpoints.
  • You can remove a buddy, leave a challenge, or sign out at any time from Settings.

Your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally. If you have signed in to Buddy and want your account or synced data deleted, contact me using the email below and I will remove it.

If new features change how data is handled, this policy will be updated.
